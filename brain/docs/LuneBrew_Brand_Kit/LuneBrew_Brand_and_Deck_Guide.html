Brand Visual Language  /  Presentation Deck Guide

Brewed in the Lune Valley, built to a system.

Everything the Lune Brew look is made of — the wordmark, the palette, the river ribbon, the stadium band — distilled into a set of rules for building on-brand slides. Use it as your reference when you make a deck.

◑ Lune = the river & the moon Bold · Rounded · Flavour-led Ink + Paper + Gold
01 — The essence

What the brand feels like

Confident craft: heritage of the Lune Valley told through clean, modern, high-contrast design.

Grounded in place

The River Lune, the valley, the aqueduct. Every product ties back to a real landscape — the brand never floats free of it.

Bold & rounded

Big condensed names, soft radii, stadium and pill shapes. Loud but friendly — approachable, not corporate.

Flavour-led colour

A disciplined ink/paper/gold core, then one saturated flavour accent per product or per deck. Colour carries the story.

Systematic

The same devices repeat with tight consistency — you recognise a Lune Brew label, or slide, in a heartbeat.

02 — Logo

The wordmark

A custom rounded-geometric wordmark. The tall stroke rising off the L reads two ways at once — a brewery chimney and the sliver of a crescent moon (lune). Keep it whole; never rebuild it in another font.

Two lockups
Stacked
Primary. Covers, badges, anywhere with room. Sits inside a thin outline circle for pump-clips & stamps.
Inline “LUNEBREW”
LUNEBREW
One-word signature used at small sizes and as the anchor inside the stadium band on packaging. (Shown here in the closest stand-in face.)
Clear space & minimums
◑
Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the “L” bowl on all sides. Minimum size: 28 px / 8 mm tall for the stacked mark. On slides, never place the logo smaller than the footer type.
Do & don’t

Do

  • Use black on light, white on dark — maximum contrast only.
  • Give it room; let the chimney stroke breathe above.
  • Place it top-left or centred on covers.

Don’t

  • Recolour it into flavour accents, add gradients, shadows or outlines.
  • Stretch, condense, rotate or re-space the letters.
  • Set it on a busy photo without an ink or paper panel behind it.

05 — Signature devices

The motif kit

These five shapes are the brand’s handwriting. Put at least one on every layout and it will read as Lune Brew — even before the logo lands.

The Lune ribbon

A single flowing river-swoosh in white (or gold). The hero device — it winds through covers, dividers and behind data. One ribbon per slide, never a tangle.

LUNEBREW

The stadium band

A thick rounded-rectangle “U”, open to one side, framing imagery or a section title. The wordmark or a label sits at its lower-left.

The label / tab card

A heavily-rounded near-black card holding structured info: name, meta, ABV, icons. Reuse it on slides as a callout or stat card.

The LB pill

A two-cell lozenge — “LB” + a number code. On packaging it’s the SKU; on decks use it to number sections or tag slides (LB 01, LB 02…).

Monoline icons & waves

Thin, single-weight pictograms — the double water-wave (≈), a globe, hops, a glass. Decorative dividers and quick visual keys. Keep them one weight, never filled.

Reversed on ink

The whole kit flips cleanly to white on Lune Ink — the brand’s default “dark mode”. Most hero slides live here.

07 — Building a deck

Rules of thumb

If you remember nothing else: Ink + Paper + one flavour accent, one motif per slide, condensed caps for headlines, and lots of room.

Do

  • Pick one flavour accent per deck (or per section) and stay with it.
  • Alternate dark hero slides with light working slides for rhythm.
  • Set headlines in condensed uppercase; keep body in Inter.
  • Number sections with the LB pill (LB 01, LB 02…).
  • Leave generous margins — ~8% padding, one idea per slide.
  • Reuse the tab card for any standout stat or callout.

Don’t

  • Use more than one flavour accent on a single slide.
  • Set body copy in the flavour accents on light backgrounds (contrast fails).
  • Crowd a slide with several ribbons, bands and cards at once.
  • Recolour, stretch or drop-shadow the logo.
  • Fall back to default office fonts or blue-grey chrome.
  • Fill the monoline icons or mix icon weights.

Pre-flight checklist

Logo correct colour for its background (white on ink, black on paper)
One flavour accent, used consistently
Headlines in condensed caps, body in Inter
At least one motif (ribbon / band / pill / card) per slide
Text contrast passes AA (ink/white only for reading)
~8% margins, one idea per slide
Closing slide with logo + “Brewed in the Lune Valley”
No default templates, clip-art or stock chrome

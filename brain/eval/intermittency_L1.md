## Note on why the corrected column is unanimous

This is not a coincidence of the estate. The KH intermittency cutoff is ADI >= 4/3, and
`2 - (3/2)(4/3) = 0` exactly, so the selection threshold is non-positive for every series
at or above the intermittency cutoff. Since CV2 >= 0 always, **any node classified
intermittent under the corrected constants necessarily selects SBA.** The two venues below
the cutoff here (Beer Hall at 1.3267 / 1.3223, Two River Taps at 1.1828) clear a small
positive threshold on their own CV2, so all six rows land on SBA either way.

The consequence is confined to the intermittent-node trigger that feeds hierarchical
reconciliation. No L1 served model is affected: those are selected by the ladder and the
Model Confidence Set, not by an intermittency label.

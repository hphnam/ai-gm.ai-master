\section{Answers to the research questions}
\label{sec:disc-answers}

Each question is answered in the order the Introduction states it; the arguments are in
Sections~\ref{sec:disc-divergences} to~\ref{sec:disc-limitations}.

The first question asked whether candidate forecasting approaches can be separated on
out-of-sample accuracy at the data volumes a three-venue estate provides, and whether the number
of evaluation origins changes which approach is selected. They cannot, and it does. The ninety
per cent confidence sets retain five of nine at the Beer Hall, four at Two River Taps and six at
Ellel (Table~\ref{tab:mcs}), separating the clearly inferior rungs from the rest but not the
foundation model from the simpler incumbents. At six origins the gate placed
the served Beer Hall model second of nine; at $273$ it returns to first and the day-of-week
baseline falls to fifth.

The second asked whether the intermittency and hierarchical structure of these venues' demand
admit the point-forecast estimand and the coherent reconciliation standard practice assumes. The
reconciliation is not admitted. The unbiasedness precondition
\citet{wickramasuriya_optimal_2019} require fails at $22$ of the Beer Hall hierarchy's $41$
nodes, $19$ of them with a positive mean residual, so the forecasts remain exactly coherent while
no minimum-variance optimality may be claimed here. All three series
classify as erratic or lumpy, none of them smooth (Table~\ref{tab:intermittency}). The estimand
survives, qualified by a served forecaster that returns a median where the decision layer needs a
mean.

The third asked whether weather, or cross-series pooling across venues, improves forecast
accuracy beyond what a venue's own trading history supports. Neither does. All five weather arms
are retained in the ninety per cent set at every venue (Table~\ref{tab:weather}), and grouping is
indistinguishable from or worse than forecasting each venue alone (Table~\ref{tab:group}). No
capability failed to engage: grouped and univariate forecasts differ by $\pounds 9.99$ to
$\pounds 10.94$ per origin at the Beer Hall and by $\pounds 185$ at the widest, so pooling moves
the forecast without moving its accuracy.

The fourth asked whether a split-conformal interval holds its nominal coverage at every venue
and, where it does not, which property of the data accounts for the shortfall. It does not, and
the property is exchangeability. Against a nominal ninety per cent the served band covers $0.871$
at the Beer Hall, $0.914$ at Ellel and $0.963$ at Two River Taps (Table~\ref{tab:coverage}).
Ranking each banded residual inside its own calibration pool reproduces all three to a
thousandth, at $0.870$, $0.913$ and $0.962$, so the shortfall is rank non-uniformity on the
objects the band is built from. Section~\ref{sec:disc-limitations} carries what it leaves
unrepaired.

The fifth asked whether, under a cost asymmetry favouring silence over noise, deviation detection
from a calibrated interval performs well enough to justify surfacing its output to an operator. On
the lag-tolerant measure the answer divides by event kind: regime shifts and exogenous
coincidences score between $0.93$ and $1.00$, spikes between $0.70$ and $0.91$
(Table~\ref{tab:vuspr}). The asymmetry itself is not resolved. Swept over
four miss-to-false-alarm ratios the weighted cost rises while $124$ misses and $8$ false alarms
stay fixed, so no operating point is selected, for the reason
Section~\ref{sec:disc-limitations} gives.

\section{Divergences from the reviewed literature}
\label{sec:disc-divergences}

Chapter~\ref{chap:litreview} marks two places where its sources point one way and this estate's
measurements point another, and defers both. Both are settled here, with four further divergences
from methods the review endorses, since a divergence left unexplained reads as an oversight.

The ranking reversal moved in a direction no cited work predicts.
\citet{hewamalage_look_2021} and \citet{brigato_there_2025} establish that rankings are unstable
under small evaluation setups and both leave the direction open: what they predict is that a
ranking may move, not which way. This estate's instance is directional and it favoured the model
already in production, which is the direction most open to the suspicion of a result arranged
after the fact. Three properties of how it arose answer that. The origin count was raised because
the review argues six origins carry almost no information, and that argument was committed before
the larger evaluation ran; the correction was applied to all nine rungs at once, not to
the incumbent alone; and the same enlarged evaluation demoted a different model from first to fifth.
The resolution is narrow. The literature predicts instability and this work observed it twice, in
opposite directions, so the direction of either movement is a fact about this sample.

Adaptive calibration performed worse than leaving the band alone. The review's conformal arc runs
through \citet{gibbs_adaptive_2021}, \citet{zaffran_adaptive_2022} and
\citet{sun_conformal_2025} toward methods that adapt their level to realised coverage, and
Table~\ref{tab:winkler} records the aggregated adaptive arm as the worst of five at every venue.
That reads as a contradiction only until the licensing condition is read, and then it inverts:
\citet{zaffran_adaptive_2022} establish that on exchangeable scores the adaptive update costs
efficiency whether or not a shift arrives. This estate had one regime change in roughly a year,
so a method paying continuously against a once-yearly event is expected to lose on aggregate. The
negative result is predicted by the cited literature rather than in tension with it, and what it
establishes is a boundary condition on when adaptivity is worth its price, one that does not
generalise past the regime-change rate producing it.

The headline measure diverges from the field's default, and the review's own chain is what
licenses it. Absolute-error measures elicit the median \citep{hewamalage_forecast_2023}, the mean
absolute scaled error is a scaled absolute error and median-eliciting point forecasts are usually
not coherent \citep{kolassa_we_2023}, and reconciliation optimality requires unbiased bases
\citep{wickramasuriya_optimal_2019}. Two arguments about measures and one condition, which the
review could state and had no way to join; Section~\ref{sec:res-reconciliation} joins them by
measuring the condition and finding it fails in the direction the first two imply.

Ellel is scored on unscaled absolute error where \citet{chatfield_all-zero_2007} licenses
replacing the objective with a cost function, so the instrument changed and the objective did
not. The remedy does not transfer: Chatfield's cost is that of an inventory system in which the
forecast is the replenishment quantity, and Ellel's estimand is daily revenue, which is neither
ordered nor held nor backordered. Its ordering, holding and shortage costs are undefined rather
than unelicited, which is a property of the estimand and not of the data that happened to be
available.

The occurrence gate diverges from its cited framework by less than this work's own record
claimed. \citet{cragg_statistical_1971} and \citet{mullahy_specification_1986} specify a fitted
binary part, and the gate was recorded as diverging because its factor takes only zero or one.
The factor is a saturated nonparametric estimator of the trading probability given day of week,
and against a saturated logit on the same dummies it reproduces the cell frequencies to within
$10^{-4}$. What fails on a deterministic calendar is the probit parameterisation, whose
coefficient does not exist under complete separation, and not the design.

The band's grouping extends its cited guarantee rather than applying it, so the attribution
splits three ways. \citet{barber_conformal_2023} supply what the construction is, a grouped
method assuming within-group exchangeability; \citet{stocker_gentle_2025} supply what it
guarantees, finite-sample marginal coverage inside each group; and \citet{sun_conformal_2025}
supply only the motivation, since a latent-state method pays a penalty scaling with its
state-prediction error and an observed closure calendar does not incur it. Their algorithm is not
implemented here, so no theorem of theirs bounds the coverage reported, and the framing of an
observed regime variable against an inferred one is this work's own.

Three smaller divergences are argued where they arise and are recorded here so the list is
complete: pooling returns a null below an estate size \citet{montero-manso_principles_2021} never
quantify, reanalysis weather is nominally the worst of the four weather arms at the Beer Hall
where the motivating mechanism puts it first, and \citet{kaas_probabilistic_2026}'s interval
finding replicates in ordering and not in magnitude.

\section{Validity of the approach}
\label{sec:disc-validity}

Three of the five answers above are non-separations, so whether the design was precise enough to
tell a real absence of difference from an instrument too blunt to find one is the question those
answers turn on. Three measurements bear on it and they point the same way.

Pairing is what makes the non-separations measurable. Every standard error quoted for a
difference between two rungs is a paired one, formed fold by fold and compared against the
independent-model quantity an unpaired comparison would use. At Ellel, on the unscaled headline
loss, pairing takes the standard deviation of the leading contrast from $\pounds 381.68$ to
$\pounds 61.51$, a factor of $6.2$. That factor is why a mean gap of $\pounds 1.91$ between the
served baseline and the argument-minimum is measurable at all: unpaired, it would sit far inside
noise, and the conclusion that the two cannot be separated would have been reached by
insufficient precision rather than by evidence.

The confidence sets are non-separable in a second and independent sense. Regenerating the
interval-calibration arms under \texttt{numpy} 1.26.4 in place of the committed 2.5.1 moves set
membership at Two River Taps, where per-step calibration is retained under the committed regime
and eliminated under the other, its exclusion $p$-value falling from $0.191$ to $0.036$. The
economical reading is that these five interval methods are not separable at this sample size: a
confidence set reporting a real distinction survives its own arithmetic, and this one does not,
which is a finding about the data rather than about the computation. The instability is confined
to set membership. Every Winkler mean, coverage figure and Clopper--Pearson limb in
Tables~\ref{tab:coverage} and~\ref{tab:winkler} reproduces exactly across both environments, and
Ellel's set is unchanged in every verdict. Two River Taps is where it surfaces because it is the
venue with the fewest evaluation origins in the estate, $205$ against $273$ and $260$, and so
carries the tightest spread between arms and the least margin to absorb a threshold crossing.

That is a second sighting of a mechanism already reported rather than a second answer to the
first question. The candidates there are forecasting approaches and these are interval methods, a
different candidate set measured in a different float, so offering one as corroboration of the
other would compare two populations. What it corroborates is the mechanism: the venue with the
fewest origins is the one whose set membership moves, and the venues with $273$ and $260$ hold.

A fourth line of evidence concerns method, not this estate. Four quantities recorded
as established in this project's own documentation did not survive recomputation, and none of the
four was identified by reasoning about it: each was identified by measuring it with enough
observations to resolve the question, and in three of them the sample size behind the original
figure had not been recorded beside it. What that licenses is narrow, and it bears directly on
the claims above: a figure whose sample size is not recorded beside it has not been verified,
which is the standard every number surviving into this chapter was held to.

\section{Limitations and threats to validity}
\label{sec:disc-limitations}

Limitations divide into properties of the problem, which would recur for anyone attempting this
work on this kind of estate, and properties of the circumstances, which would not. They are
separated because conflating them lets a provisioning failure read as a research finding, or the
reverse.

Four are properties of the problem. The served forecaster returns a median under a mean's name:
this work adopts a squared-scaled headline on the argument that the decision layer needs a
mean-eliciting measure, and the model in production cannot emit a mean. Integrating over the
emitted quantiles is the available remedy and was declined because it would change the served
model after the evaluation was frozen. The reconciliation's optimality does not hold, for the
reason Section~\ref{sec:res-reconciliation} measures. Ellel's occurrence signal exists in the world and not in
the dataset, so the hurdle cannot be tested as a hurdle at the one venue whose occurrence is
genuinely uncertain, and the circular substitute of deriving occurrence from that venue's own
trading history is unreachable by any code path. The cost of that absence has been measured:
$1037$ of Ellel's $1300$ calendar-open days did not trade, and the venue's entire residual drift
sits on those days, none of it on the $263$ days it did trade. Fourth, the
Mondrian partition's day-of-week calendar can fail in either direction and this estate supplies
one venue of each: the Beer Hall's calendar calls $546$ days closed and $94$ of them traded,
while Ellel's calls $1300$ open and $1037$ did not. No recency weighting repairs a group
specified wrongly, which is why a shorter calibration memory brings one venue to nominal and
moves another away from it.

Four are properties of the circumstances, and the first is the largest single gap between what
this work set out to do and what it reports. The intervention layer's apparatus is complete,
tested and frozen by commit ordering, and it has not been run, because executing it requires live
model calls on a production integration outside this work's control; three of the four terms of
its objective compute as soon as a response cache exists. The expected calibration error the
review identifies as the instrument capable of carrying a coverage guarantee through to an
agent's output \citep{guo_calibration_2017} is implemented and never run for the same reason. The
fourth term requires human accept-or-dismiss judgements, of which none were obtained, so no
agreement statistic exists and the judge remains an uncalibrated proxy
\citep{zheng_judging_2023, bavaresco_llms_2025}; a fallback protocol of repeated self-annotation
is specified, and reporting it as equivalent to independent annotation would be an over-claim
that is not made. The cost ratio fixing the weight in the surfacing measure was never elicited
\citep{fu_prism_2026}, which is why the sweep reports a curve and selects no point on it. And
cross-venue transfer is not evaluable rather than unevaluated: the test requires three venues
carrying a defensible scaled error and this estate has two.

Three biases are disclosed rather than mitigated, because none can be removed at this scale. The
evaluation of surfaced insights had one rater and that rater is the author, so no inter-rater
statistic is available. The literature review was written after much of the experimental work it
introduces, so where a cited source anticipates an outcome here that anticipation is an argument
a reader can check against the source, not a record of a prior belief. And the corpus
every result rests on was assembled by the same work that then evaluated on it, for the reason
Section~\ref{sec:disc-specification} gives; that is a threat to external validity, and it is
repeated here because it is also one to internal validity.

Four assumptions carry the results and each has a stated consequence if it fails.
Exchangeability of the conformity scores is assumed by the interval construction and is measured
to fail at two venues, and that measurement is one of this work's findings. Day of week stands
in for occurrence, and the two-directional partition defect above is the cost of that
substitution. The detection figures rest on a synthetic injection corpus standing in for events
an operator confirmed, so the $124$ misses are counted against injections and not against
operational reality, which bounds what the cost sweep establishes. And the coverage intervals
assume independent indicators, which overlapping origins at a seven-day horizon do not supply, so
those intervals are mildly optimistic and are reported as such.

Two omissions in the review itself belong here and not only in the appendix recording them. The
corpus search was not pre-registered and no protocol document exists, so
Appendix~\ref{app:search} records criteria alone. No screened-versus-retained
count exists at any stage and none is reconstructed, which is why no flow diagram is drawn:
drawing one would put a claim this work's own evidence contradicts into the document's most
legible form.

\section{Divergence from the project specification}
\label{sec:disc-specification}

Six things the project specification describes did not hold for the work as done. They are stated
together because the pattern across them is informative: four of the six are methods this review
argues for and the specification undertook to supply the inputs to, so the reduction in scope
followed from inputs that did not arrive rather than from judgements about what was worth
attempting.

The specification offers read access to a dedicated research schema in the production database
from the first week. No such schema was provisioned at any point, so every result here rests on a
corpus assembled from source exports into a local warehouse. That is the divergence with the
widest consequence: the external validity of every figure is untested, because the data was
prepared by the same work that evaluated on it and no independently provisioned copy exists
against which the preparation could be checked.

The specification describes the platform as live across four venues and this study covers three.
The two statements are not in conflict, and the distinction is worth stating precisely. Four
counts the platform's deployment; three is the study's sample, and the difference between them is
the unit of analysis rather than an exclusion. A trading venue here is a fixed site with an
opening calendar, against which a daily rhythm can be learned and a departure from it defined.
The fourth location books off-site events, so it has no site and no opening calendar and
therefore no rhythm to learn, whatever it turns over. That is a boundary on what the study is
about, not a threshold applied to data, so no cut-off was set and none is reported.

Three named deliverables were not reached. An agent reasoning over the deviation signals and
deciding what to raise exists, is frozen, and has not been measured, for the reason
Section~\ref{sec:disc-limitations} gives. Qualitative manager feedback is named among the
evaluation deliverables and none was obtained, so the term of the objective requiring human
judgement is unmeasured rather than measured badly. Multi-venue transfer learning, which the
specification calls the interesting research, is blocked by the estate's own composition, for the
reason Section~\ref{sec:disc-limitations} gives.

The sixth is a licensing constraint, and it is recorded because any comparable project would
meet it. A tabular foundation model this work argues is
regime-appropriate \citep{hollmann_accurate_2025} was entered and withdrawn: its current weights
require accepting a licence permitting testing, evaluation and internal benchmarking while
prohibiting the use of model outputs as inputs to internal commercial decision-making
\citep{grinsztajn_tabpfn-3_2026}. This estate is a trading business with an operating decision
layer, so evaluating that model is licensed and serving it is not, on terms no credential
changes. The regime-fit argument is accordingly stated for both model families and evidenced for
one, and nothing here is claimed about how the withdrawn candidate would have performed.

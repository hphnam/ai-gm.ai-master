## The question, in one sentence

**What is a 20,000-word body expected to contain for a project whose marking criteria mandate the
same measurement in three separate chapters?**

## The conflict, stated precisely

Three groups of criteria each mandate in-body content, and for a measurement-dense project they
compound rather than overlap:

- **R83 and R84** — *"Methods justify why each decision was made"* and *"why the alternatives
  considered were rejected"*. Every rejected alternative's reason must be in Chapter 3.
- **R102** — *"Results state what the findings imply for the research question(s)"*. Every
  finding, its number and its implication must be in Chapter 4. A cross-reference to an appendix
  does not discharge it.
- **R103** — *"The Discussion answers what the results reveal in relation to the research
  question(s)"*. The same findings must be answered again in Chapter 5.

**R102 and R103 are close to the same requirement mandated in two chapters.** In the current
draft §5.1 restates Chapter 4's confidence-set sizes, its origin-count reversal, its
standard-error figures and its node counts — with the numbers — because both criteria ask for
them. We have taken the position that R102's minimum is the finding and its number, with the
consequence left to R103, and priced the saving. That is an interpretation, and **confirming or
correcting it is the first thing worth asking.**

**The measured cost of the demand this creates.** Demoting material to an appendix saves, per
word moved, according to which criterion governs the passage:

| Criterion governing the passage | Realised saving |
|---|---|
| None | 46 – 66 % |
| R83/R84 (a *reason* is one sentence) | 84 % |
| **R102 (a finding, its number and its consequence)** | **6 – 30 %** |

So the length is not distributed evenly across the document. **It concentrates exactly where the
criteria demand the most in-body content**, which is Results, and Results is where this project's
contribution sits.

---

## What we are asking

1. **Is our reading of R102's minimum correct** — that Results may state the finding and its
   number and leave the consequence to the Discussion's answer under R103?
2. **Does the 20,000 limit contemplate a project with this measurement density**, and if so, what
   is expected to give? The realistic candidates are: fewer reported results, a narrower research
   question set, or acceptance that a measurement-dense project runs long.
3. **If material must leave the document entirely**, we would want that framed as a scope decision
   about what the dissertation claims rather than an editorial one about length. Each candidate is
   a result that survived the audit passes, and choosing which findings are not reported changes
   what the work asserts.
4. **Are the appendices read and assessed?** The whole reduction strategy assumes they are. Every
   derivation, every reconciliation working and every rejected alternative now sits in Appendices
   A to E, with the body carrying the finding and a cross-reference. If appendices are excluded
   from the word count *and* not read closely, then what we have been doing is moving evidence out
   of the marker's view rather than out of the count, which would be a worse outcome than being
   over length. We would rather know now than after submission.

---

## What we are not asking for

Not an extension, and not permission to exceed the limit. The question is what a compliant body
is expected to contain, so that whatever leaves the document leaves for a stated reason.

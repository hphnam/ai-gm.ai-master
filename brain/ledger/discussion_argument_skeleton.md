## The one correction this skeleton makes to its own hand-off

**D10's factor is 6.2 on the committed headline loss, not the 6.6 the hand-off names, and the
gap is £1.91, not £1.55.** Both triples are correct; they are on **different losses**, and only
one of them is the loss Chapter 4 reports.

|---|---|---|
| loss at Ellel | **MAE** (secondary) | **RMSE** (headline, `headline_loss_at_venue`) |
| served / argument-minimum | 107.59 / 106.04 | — |
| paired mean gap | **£1.55** | **£1.91** |
| paired sd | **26.94** | **61.51** |
| unpaired sd | **178.00** | **381.68** |
| pairing factor | **6.6** | **6.2049** |
| gap in paired se | 0.93 (se 1.67) | **0.50** (se 3.8149) |

`log/70` §10 is the Gate A rescore and is right about MAE. `sec:res-mcs-functional` as composed
reports **£1.91 against £3.81, which is 0.50** standard errors, matching the artefact. So writing
6.6 and £1.55 into 5.3 would put a secondary-loss figure beside a chapter reporting the headline
loss, and would state a gap Chapter 4 does not report. **5.3 uses the headline-loss triple and
names the basis**, per the rule that a scaled or unscaled figure is ambiguous without it. The
argument is unchanged: 6.2 licenses the null exactly as 6.6 would.

This is the FLAG-MASE-RULER family recurring one level up again, and it was caught the way the
rules prescribe: by opening the artefact rather than by trusting a number that had been carried
through three files. `numbers_audit.md` has **no row** for 178.00, 26.94 or 6.6, so the figure was
never audited; it is not a MISMATCH being ignored, it is an unaudited number that a basis change
overtook.

## Two things this chapter's composition establishes that were open

**06 §9's open question is answered, and the answer is BOUNDARY.** `06_research_questions.md` §9
records that nothing in `brain/` says *why* the estate is three venues rather than the
specification's four, and hands the question to 8D with a conditional: if it was an exclusion,
Methods 3.1 owes a criterion. `config.py:110–137` answers it. The fourth Square location is
`events`, an off-site event-booking location with no site and no opening calendar, and the comment
states the reasoning in the required terms: *"This is a BOUNDARY on what the study is about, not a
threshold applied to data — so there is no cut-off here, and none was ever set."* The unit of
analysis is a trading venue. **The conditional does not fire and Methods 3.1 owes nothing.**
The same comment forbids restating the location's volume (203 line items) as the reason, because a
volume rationale reads as a data-driven threshold and invites the question when it was set. 5.5
follows that instruction.

**The specification's "4 venues" is a claim about the platform, not about the study's sample.**
`docs/PRJ93.md` reads *"Currently live across 4 Lune Brew Co venues"* in a description of the
product. It nowhere specifies a four-venue study. So the divergence is a platform-versus-study
distinction and is stated as one.
